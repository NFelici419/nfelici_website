Good to Know

Questions

How do I book my date?

A signed contract and a 30% deposit hold your date. All payments are non-refundable.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. After the 30% deposit, the rest can be split into monthly payments, with the balance paid in full two weeks before the wedding.

When will I get my photos?

Sneak peeks arrive within 2–3 weeks, and your full gallery within 2–4 months (6 weeks with The Full Story). Want something to post right away? The next-day social media preview gets you 20–30 edits the day after.

Do you travel?

Travel to Bloomington and Indianapolis is included in every package. Anywhere else is $0.50 per mile.
